# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project using **Python, SQL Server, Power BI, and DAX** to analyze 3,900 customer transactions, uncover purchasing patterns, evaluate customer segments, and generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to understand customer shopping behavior and identify insights that can support better business decisions.

The analysis focuses on:
- Customer purchasing and spending patterns.
- Revenue contribution across customer demographics.
- Customer loyalty and subscription behavior.
- Product preferences and customer ratings.
- Discount usage and shipping preferences.
- Customer segmentation based on purchase history.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 3,900 customer purchase records and 18 columns.

**Key Features:**

- Customer demographics: Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status.
- Purchase information: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color.
- Shopping behavior: Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type.

**Data Quality:**
- Total records: 3,900
- Total columns: 18
- Missing values: 37 in the Review Rating column.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

Used Python and Pandas to prepare the raw dataset for analysis.

**Key activities:**

- Imported and explored the dataset using Pandas.
- Used `df.info()` and `df.describe()` for initial data exploration.
- Identified missing values and handled 37 missing review ratings using category-wise median imputation.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` column using age binning.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature.
- Checked redundancy between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used`.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for SQL analysis.

### 2. Business Analysis (SQL Server)

Developed and executed 10 SQL business queries to investigate customer behavior and answer important business questions.

| No. | Business Question | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue by Gender | Compare total revenue generated by male and female customers. |
| 2 | High-Spending Discount Users | Identify customers who used discounts but spent above the average purchase amount. |
| 3 | Top 5 Products by Rating | Find products with the highest average review ratings. |
| 4 | Shipping Type Comparison | Compare average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping. |
| 5 | Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers | Compare average spending and total revenue by subscription status. |
| 6 | Discount-Dependent Products | Identify five products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases. |
| 7 | Customer Segmentation | Classify customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments. |
| 8 | Top 3 Products per Category | Find the three most-purchased products within each category. |
| 9 | Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions | Examine whether customers with more than five purchases are more likely to subscribe. |
| 10 | Revenue by Age Group | Calculate revenue contribution across different age groups. |

### 3. Interactive Dashboard Development (Power BI)

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize customer shopping behavior and business performance.

**DAX Measures Developed**

Created eight DAX measures to calculate key performance indicators and support business analysis.

| DAX Measure | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Calculates overall revenue generated from purchases. |
| Total Customers | Measures the number of customers. |
| Total Purchases | Calculates the total number of purchases. |
| Average Purchase Value | Measures average spending per purchase. |
| Average Review Rating | Evaluates average customer product ratings. |
| Revenue per Customer | Measures average revenue generated per customer. |
| Discount Usage % | Measures the percentage of purchases using discounts. |
| Subscriber % | Calculates the percentage of subscribed customers. |

These measures help present customer purchasing patterns, revenue performance, discount usage, and subscription behavior through interactive visualizations.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations were identified:

**1. Boost Subscriptions**

Promote exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage customers to subscribe.

**2. Customer Loyalty Programs**

Reward repeat buyers and encourage them to move into the Loyal customer segment.

**3. Review Discount Policy**

Balance discount-driven sales growth with profit margin control.

**4. Product Positioning**

Highlight top-rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.

**5. Targeted Marketing**

Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue age groups and customers using express shipping.
